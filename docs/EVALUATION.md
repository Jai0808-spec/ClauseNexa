It does not check logic or negation, so treat it as an upper bound and spot-check failures. Citation precision is low by design when 5 chunks are returned for a one-clause question.

## Limitations

- Four small fictional contracts and about 45 questions: a smoke-level benchmark, not a statistically strong one.
- Gold labels were written by one person (the assistant) and should be reviewed by the team.
- Faithfulness is lexical, not an LLM or NLI judge.
- The risk rules are keyword rules, so wording they were never given (see misses) is the main failure mode.
